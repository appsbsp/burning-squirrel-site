Backlog · Privacy Policy

Privacy

The short version: your projects, releases, and items live in your iCloud, not ours. We built the app so there was never anything of yours to collect.

On Your Device

Backlog keeps its own copy of your projects, releases, and items in the app's private storage area on your iPhone or iPad — nowhere else on the device, and nowhere the app can be reached from except by you opening it.

A handful of small preferences — things like appearance and sync mode — are stored the same way, using Apple's standard UserDefaults mechanism. These are settings, not content: none of them describe you, and none of them leave the device except as part of an iCloud device backup you control.

Your Private iCloud

If you leave sync turned on, each of your projects, releases, and items is also mirrored to your own private CloudKit database — container iCloud.com.burningsquirrelproductions.Backlog — the same private-by-default storage Apple gives every app, tied to your Apple ID. It is not a shared or public database: only devices signed into your own iCloud account can read it, the same way only your devices can read your iCloud Photos or iCloud Drive files.

We — the developer — have no access to this database. There is no server of ours in the loop, no copy on our infrastructure, and no way for us to see a single one of your projects, releases, and items. Apple's standard iCloud security and encryption protects it the same way it protects everything else in your iCloud account.

What We Never Do

No account or sign-in
No analytics or crash reporting
No advertising or ad SDKs
No third-party services of any kind
Nothing sold, shared, or sent anywhere on our behalf

Your Control

Settings → iCloud lets you switch to local only at any time. Doing so deletes Backlog's iCloud copy of your projects, releases, and items (after you confirm) and stops syncing from then on — your projects, releases, and items stay only on this device.

Deleting the app removes its on-device copy along with it. There's no "delete my account" step, because there was never an account to begin with.

The Fine Print

Children

Backlog is not directed at children and isn't designed to collect information from anyone — which, given everything above, is true regardless of who's using it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — say, because a future version adds a feature that touches data differently — this page will be updated and the date below will move.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can go to apps@burningsquirrelproductions.com.
