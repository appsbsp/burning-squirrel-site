Curated

The list of everything you meant to read, watch, listen to, and live — finally in one place, and finally on all your devices.

Get Notified At Launch See The Whole Portfolio

What It Is

Curated is a life backlog tracker. Create color-coded categories — it ships with Books, Movies, TV Shows, and Music, but any category is yours to define — and file items under them with a creator, a status of future, in progress, or complete, an optional target year, and a completion date. The home screen is a grid of category tiles; tap one to open it.

Screenshots

Key Features

iCloud sync, built in

The same backlog on iPhone and iPad, with a live sync status screen so you can watch it happen.

Categories, your way

Color-coded categories aren't limited to a fixed list of media types — make one for anything you track.

Three-state progress

Future, in progress, complete — plus an optional target year for the readers and watchers who set annual goals.

Built to last

Automatic duplicate-category merging, orphaned-item recovery, and a full store reset, backed by a real test suite.

Pricing

Free to start, one-time unlock for sync

Curated is free with a single local category. A one-time Curated Pro unlock adds unlimited categories and iCloud sync across every device you own — no subscription, ever.
