What It Is

Quotable is a quote keeper. Save a quote with its author, source title, and source type — book, article, speech, podcast, film, song, poem, interview, or conversation — alongside a private note on why it mattered to you. Organize quotes into color-accented categories, favorite the ones you return to, and let a home-screen widget resurface them.

Key Features

A widget that actually gets used

A home-screen widget rotates a new quote every hour, drawing from everything, favorites only, or one category.

Why it mattered

A private note on each quote keeps the context, not just the words.

Proper attribution

Ten source types with correct formatting, so anything you share is credited right.

One-tap sharing

Clean, quotation-mark-correct copy and share, every time.
