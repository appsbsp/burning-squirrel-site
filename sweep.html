What It Is

Sweep is a Gmail app built in the spirit of Google's retired Inbox. Mail groups itself into bundles, the important details float up as highlight cards right in the list, and a swipe marks a thread done. Reminders live in the inbox alongside your mail, so the to-do and the email that caused it sit together.

Key Features

Bundles

Updates, promos, and the rest gather into bundles you can open, skim, and sweep away in one move.

Highlights in the list

Orders, trips, and events show their key facts as cards before you open a thing.

Done, pinned, reminded

Swipe right for done, star to pin, and add reminders that also show up in Apple's Reminders.

Straight to Gmail

Talks to Gmail directly from your device. No middleman server reading your mail.
