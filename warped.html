What It Is

Warped is a Metal-powered photo warping editor. Stack warp layers on a photo, drag an anchor point directly on the image to place each effect, and tune it with a small, shared set of controls. Every value is normalized, so the fast on-screen preview is exactly what exports at full 12MP.

Key Features

Nine warp effects

Fisheye, Twirl, Sphere, Ripple, Wave, Slice, Taper, Wrap, and Kaleido.

Stack and reorder

Combine effects, reorder them, and swap one out without losing your anchor or sizing.

Shuffle

Roll a random warp tuned to always produce a usable result, never a no-op or a wrecked frame.

Full-resolution, real-time

A custom Metal pipeline previews live and exports at the same resolution-independent fidelity.
